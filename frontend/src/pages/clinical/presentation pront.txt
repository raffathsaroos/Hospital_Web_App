- Purpose: reduce crowded, rushed, paper-based hospital workflows through organized appointment scheduling, digital patient management, role-based work queues, and connected clinical records.

- Architecture: React client → REST API → routes → middleware → controllers → services → DAOs → Mongoose models → MongoDB.

- Important business rule: a doctor’s appointment slot supports a maximum of 10 active patients.
- Appointment lifecycle: Pending → Confirmed → Paid → Diagnosed, with controlled Rejected and Cancelled transitions.
